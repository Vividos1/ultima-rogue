HOW TO PLAY THE DEMO

Explore a seeded UO-style dungeon. This is a movement and environment prototype, not yet a combat-enabled release.

Left-click on floors to pathfind there. Double-click doors, corpses, chests or stairs to interact. Right-click and hold to steer from your character, like Classic UO.

MOUSE MOVEMENT move eight directions. E interacts with the nearest object, Esc closes windows, Mouse wheel zooms. Tab switches panel.

Open the skeleton in your cell, take its starting gear, and walk through the unlocked cell door. Find chests and the stairs to explore the next generated floor.

Seeds are repeatable for each version. You can paste a seed and choose New Dungeon to replay it.
